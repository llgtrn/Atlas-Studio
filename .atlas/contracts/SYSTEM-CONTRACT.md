# Atlas Studio System Contract

The following are hard invariants and SHALL become machine-enforced Genome rules.

## Identity and truth

- `ATLAS_ROOT_DIRECTORY_IS_DOT_ATLAS`
- `ATLAS_BINARY_ARTIFACT_IS_NOT_ATLAS_ROOT_DIRECTORY`
- `GENOME_HASH_REQUIRED`
- `PROVENANCE_REQUIRED`
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS_REQUIRED`
- `REBUILDABLE_PROJECTION_IS_NOT_TRUTH`
- `AI_OUTPUT_IS_CANDIDATE_UNTIL_VERIFIED`
- `INGESTION_IS_NOT_EXECUTION`

## Graph

- `UNIVERSAL_GRAPH_SPINE_REQUIRED`
- `GLOBAL_STABLE_IDENTITY_REQUIRED`
- `NODE_EDGE_BINDING_DISTINCT`
- `TEMPORAL_REVISION_REQUIRED_FOR_MUTABLE_MEANING`
- `EVIDENCE_LINK_REQUIRED_FOR_IMPORTANT_CLAIMS`
- `CROSS_REPO_BINDING_MUST_REFERENCE_SOVEREIGN_IDENTITY`
- `PRIVATE_REPO_GRAPH_UNIVERSE_FORBIDDEN`
- `DOMAIN_EXTENSIONS_MAY_EXTEND_NOT_REPLACE_CORE_GRAPH`

## Census and planning

- `CENSUS_SCOPE_LATTICE_REQUIRED`
- `ADAPTIVE_DEPTH_REQUIRED`
- `GLOBAL_LOCAL_ATOMIC_PLAN_REQUIRED`
- `SOURCE_REALITY_AND_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE_DISTINCT`
- `CONFLICT_IS_PRESERVED_NOT_SILENTLY_RESOLVED`

## Formats

- `ATLAS_PRECEDES_ATLASX`
- `ATLAS_IS_DENSE_BINARY_ENGINEERING_ARTIFACT`
- `ATLASX_IS_EXPANDED_EXECUTABLE_REPOSITORY_REPRESENTATION`
- `ATLASX_IS_NOT_MARKDOWN_OR_JSON_AUTHORITY`
- `ATLAS_TO_ATLASX_IS_DETERMINISTIC_FOR_PINNED_INPUTS`
- `FORMAT_VERSION_AND_INTEGRITY_REQUIRED`
- `RANDOM_ACCESS_AND_BOUNDED_DECODE_REQUIRED`

## Materialization and compiler

- `BACKEND_BOOTSTRAP_IS_RUST`
- `FRONTEND_BOOTSTRAP_IS_TYPESCRIPT`
- `C_IS_BOUNDARY_BY_DEFAULT`
- `GENERATED_SOURCE_IS_MATERIALIZATION_NOT_CANONICAL_MEANING`
- `OPTIMIZATION_MUST_PRESERVE_GRAPH_INVARIANTS`
- `NATIVE_TECHNOLOGY_CANNOT_REQUIRE_DONOR_RUNTIME`
- `VERIFY_AND_RECENSUS_AFTER_MATERIALIZATION`

ATLAS readers validate magic, version, genome identity, bounds, offsets, chunk hashes and root integrity before trusting encoded counts or references. Artifact publication is transactional. Secrets and unsafe donor content are not silently persisted.

The current `atlas.systemizer.cli.v1` surface is compatibility only.
